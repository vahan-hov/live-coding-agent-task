# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per calendar year, accrued
monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month. PTO begins accruing on the employee's
first day of employment.

Unused PTO of up to 5 days may be carried over into the following calendar year.
Any balance above 5 days that is unused by December 31 is forfeited.

To request PTO, submit a request through the HR portal at least two weeks in
advance for absences longer than three days. Manager approval is required for all
PTO requests.

Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO and does not count against the PTO
balance. Employees receive 10 sick days per year.
