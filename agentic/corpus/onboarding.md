# New Hire Onboarding Guide

Welcome aboard. On your first day, collect your laptop and security badge from
the IT desk on the third floor. Your manager will share the team's onboarding
checklist and assign you an onboarding buddy.

In your first week you should: complete the security and compliance training,
set up MFA on all internal systems, join your team's communication channels, and
schedule one-on-ones with your immediate teammates.

Health insurance and 401(k) enrollment must be completed within 30 days of your
start date through the benefits portal. The company matches 401(k)
contributions up to 4 percent of salary.

Your first performance check-in happens at the 90-day mark. There is no formal
probation period, but the check-in is used to confirm role expectations.
