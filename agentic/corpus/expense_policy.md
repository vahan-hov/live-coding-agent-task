# Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees may expense reasonable business travel costs, including airfare,
lodging, ground transportation, and meals. Airfare should be booked in economy
class for flights under six hours.

The daily meal allowance (per diem) is 75 USD for domestic travel and 100 USD
for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Receipts are required for any single expense over 25 USD. Submit expense reports
through the finance portal within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Reports
submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed.

Reimbursements are processed on the next payroll cycle after manager and finance
approval, typically within two weeks.
